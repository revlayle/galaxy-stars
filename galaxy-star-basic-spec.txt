Another single-screen video game called - Galaxy Stars

This one features the player as a alien.
The player can freely move around the screen
Pressing the button boosts the player to sprint

Each level is a called a galaxy
Each level features a multiple stationary stars
Stars, when slammed into by the player, will eventually super nova, causing a small explosion.

The goal is to clear all the level's stars and move to the next galaxy
Stars, eventually activate and become hostile stars and seek out the player.
The player can sprint to slam a star to destroy it.  Stars that have become hostile do not explode like a super nova

When the player touches a stationary star
Depending on the speed it was hit will increase a super nova counter.
At slow speed it increases by one.
At sprint speed it increases by three.
At 3 or more the start will being the super nova process. It will xplode in 2 seconds
If the player is caught in the explosion, the lose a life.
If a hostile star is caught in the explosions, it is destroyed and the player gets points
If a nearby stationary star is caught in the explosion, it will cause those stationary stars to immediately begin the super nova process (essentially adding 3 to the star's super nova counter)

When the player hits a stationary star the will bounce back.
When the player uses boost to hit a hostile star they bascially will propel the hostile star off the screen.  Any other hostile star they hit also gets destroyed and propelled off screen

Player starts the game with 3 lives.
The game tracks a score multiplier it starts at x1.  All scoring events are always multipled by this number.
Each galaxy has 60 seconds of time.
Multiplier resets to x1 and the end of a level.

Scoring:
- Causing a star to super nova = 100 points and +1 multiplier
- A super nova destroying a hostile star = 500 - every other hostiale start they can hit in a chain will double scroe (independently of the multiplier)
- A level is complete when all starts stationary or hostile are destroyed.
- Level complete bonus = 100 * time reamining
- The player can continue to 0 time, nothing bad happens, just no bonus is given.
- Extra lives ever 10,000 points.
- Multiplier can never go beyond x9
- Number of player stock cannot go beyond 9.
- Player loses a life if
  - Touched by a hostile star and the player is not boost moving
  - Caught in a supernova explosion
- Multiplier, when more than x1 will eventually drop by 1 over time until it is back to x1.
  - at x9 it will drop to x8 after 1 second
  - at x2 it will drop to x1 after 3.5 seconds
  - a multiplier increasing event resets the timer.  At x9 a multipler event does not increase to x10 but will reset the timer.
  
Playe field:
- Top Row = score + level + multipler + lives
- The rest of the playfield = 2x2 tiles or the remaining 40 cols by 24 rows = 20 x 12 playfield
  
More game play notes
- At level 1, the hostile star appears every 6 seconds.  Every level it drops by half a second, until a floor over every one second.
- The player can have a maximum 6 hostile stars chasing them.
- Level 1 has 15 stars on screen, increase by 2 stars every level until a max of 45 stars.
- 1 second before a star goes hostile, it flashes red for a second, the stationary star then turns into a hostile red star to chase the player, the original stationary star is gone.
- Supernovas are explosions that occupy the 3x3 tile around the star the super novaed - again surrounding stationary stars immediate start their super nova count down
- There is a 1 in 100 chance that a super nova leaves behind a quasar - the quasar burns out ofter 5 seconds.  If the player grabs it, they are invincible for 8 seconds.
  - During invicibility, player will constantly move at boost speed, no button press required
  - Any stationary star they touch immediately super novas
  - Any hostile star is immediately destroyed (counts as part of a hostile star chain)
  - quasars can only appear once a level
- Players always appear in the center
  - No stationary stars can appear immediately around them
- Enemy stars on level 1 move approximately half of the player's normal speed
- By level 11 they should be moving about 120% player speed. Player will have to boost to get away

Movement notes:
- Pressing the button boosts 
  - the player moves 2.5x speed for 1.5 seconds
  - Player has full control
  - player cannot boost again for a half a second until after the boost ends
